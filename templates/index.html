    About

    Why EcoWise?

    Every search is an opportunity to choose better. EcoWise scores products on sustainability signals from their listing like materials, durability cues, and certifications, and combines that with what real shoppers think. The result: confident, mindful picks in seconds.

    • Hybrid ranking (relevance + eco + rating)
    • Smart search routing (e.g. "phone" goes to Smartphones)
    • Transparent eco-score badges on every product
    • Fast, responsive, and mobile-first
